Frequently Asked Questions

 
  • Why is the form not working?

    • You need to fill all minimum required fields in order for
    the form to be sent to PRO PRINT.


  • Why is my building number not listed in the 'Delivery Bldg #' field' ?

    • If you are located in Silicon Valley, orders are delivered to
    your lobby. Orders from other areas are shipped via FedEx
    to your physical address.


  • What if I’m unsure of the delivery date?

    • If no delivery date is checked, we always assume a standard
    10 -business day turn-around to be sufficient.
    If you need your cards sooner, check either the 50% or
    100% box. In addition, we ask that you give us an actual
    date in the “RUSH needed by” field.


  • I would like to get an order confirmation. Can you call me?

    • By checking the box “If you would like a call confirming your
    order was received...”, you can be sure to get a confirmation call

  • I have a translation from a previous order. How do I get it to you?

    • If you are ordering a name (or entire card order) printed before,
    please fax BOTH sides of the old card in the same language right
    after you have submitted the on-line order to 650-249-0436. 
    Also, if your name is of Chinese, Japanese or Korean descent,
    we need to know your name’s characters.  Unless you provide
    them to us (by separate fax), we assume that the characters are
    not known to you, and the name will be literally translated (not
    recommended).


  • I don’t see my address in the drop-down menu. What do I do?

    • Addresses in the drop-down menu are the ones we most
    commonly see ordered. If your company address is not
    mentioned, please fill it in completely under “Other Address” right
    after the Mail Stop field.

    Anything that we need to know in order to process your order,
    and/orif your cards need to be shipped to a different address
    other than the one that will print on your card, please indicate in
    "Comment" area (free flow).

    If at any time during the process you have questions, please contact customer
        service at (650) 670-2405 or by e-mail to support@amatorders.com.
